About MySemester
Student Workspace

Everything for your semester, kept in one clean dashboard.

MySemester helps students organize courses, folders, grades, assessments, files, GPA scales, and planning tools without turning school tracking into another messy spreadsheet.

What It Handles

Courses and folders

Create courses, group them into folders, and see folder counts at a glance.

Grade tracking

Add weighted assessments, estimate target scores, and track GPA plus course average.

University settings

Choose or change your university anytime and use school-specific GPA scale references.

Notes and files

Keep course notes, links, files, and details close to the class they belong to.

Planning Tools

AI Planner

Build course plans, explain courses, browse the catalog, and prepare smarter semester decisions.

Professor Picks

Test feature for UofT course searches with RMP (Rate My Professors) rating cards and clean comparisons.

Mobile polish

Inputs stay readable on phones without unwanted zoom, and empty states are built for small screens.

Account sync

Sign in to keep your semester workspace available across devices.

Some tools are still experimental.

AI Planner and Professor Picks are meant as helpers. Always verify enrolment, graduation, instructor, and GPA decisions with official university sources.
